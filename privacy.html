Family Archive: Privacy Policy

Family Archive is a private tool used only by Ben Lewis to copy and organize his own files into his own Google Drive. It is not offered to the public.

It accesses Google Drive data only for the signed-in owner's account, and only to upload, organize, and verify the owner's own files. No data is collected, stored elsewhere, shared, sold, or sent to any third party.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
